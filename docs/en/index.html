Pause Internet for each device at home, from your phone

Without turning WiFi off for everyone: the console, the tablet or the laptop, with one button, with schedules or with alerts when they connect.

AndroidCode on GitHub
⏸️

One-button pause

Pick the devices and cut their Internet. Another button brings it back.

⏰

Automatic schedules

«Monday to Friday, 21:00 to 8:00, no Internet»: the app does it by itself.

🔔

Connection alerts

It alerts you when a specific device connects, or a new one you don't know.

🧾

Your router, everyone's

It talks directly to the router. If yours isn't supported, you can teach it and share it.

🔒

Private

Passwords are stored encrypted on your phone. No accounts, no ads, no servers.

🎬

Demo mode

Try it without touching your router, with sample devices.

How it works

  1. Install the app and choose your router model (or read it from the sticker with a photo).
  2. Enter the router's admin password. It's stored encrypted, only on your phone.
  3. Name the devices, add them to the pause and create the schedules you want.

Routers

The app includes an open catalog of routers from Spanish providers, completed by the users themselves.
