Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Who publishes it

WiFi Time Control is a free-software app published by Makirfer. Contact: wifitimecontrol@gmail.com. Code: https://github.com/Makirfer/wificontrol.

Summary

The app has no accounts, ads, analytics or servers of its own. We don't sell or share data with anyone. The only exception is technical diagnostic data from Google's text reader, explained below.

Data stored on your phone

  • Your router's address, username and admin password: the password is stored encrypted with the Android Keystore.
  • Names of locations and devices (with their MAC and IP address), the pause, schedules and alerts you set up.
  • The lock PIN, if you enable it: only an irreversible salted SHA-256 hash is stored, never the PIN.
This data never leaves the phone and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Connections the app makes

  • Your router, on your local network, to list devices and cut or restore their Internet.
  • GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com), to download the public catalog of routers and recipes. None of your data is sent; GitHub may log the IP address like any website (see its privacy policy).

Camera and photos

If you use «Read the sticker with a photo», the image is processed on the phone with Google ML Kit text and QR code recognition, offline, and deleted afterwards. The app doesn't store or send photos or the text read. When the reader is used, Google ML Kit sends Google technical diagnostic and usage data (phone model and OS, app name and version, a per-installation identifier that doesn't identify the person, timings and errors), encrypted, as described in its data disclosure.

Feedback and reports

If you tap «Send feedback» or «Send report», the app you choose (email, WhatsApp…) opens with the text ready, and you send it yourself. It includes the app version, phone model and router; never passwords or the list of your devices. Each device's brand comes from its MAC address using a public list built into the app, offline.

Learning mode and recordings

When teaching a new router, the app records what its panel does. Before saving, passwords, WiFi keys and other secrets are removed. The recording stays on your phone and only leaves it if you choose to share it (for example, with an AI to create the recipe).

Publishing to the catalog

If you choose to publish a recipe or report a router, GitHub opens with a form that you submit with your own GitHub account. What you publish is public; it contains no passwords.

Notifications and permissions

The app asks for notifications (connection alerts), exact alarms (schedules), boot completed (to reschedule) and fingerprint/face (optional lock). None is used to collect data.

Children

The app is used by adults to manage their network. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, it will be published here with the new date.
